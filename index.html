Meet Jay Reed

Jay Reed | Owner & Licensed Master HVAC Mechanic

With more than 30 years of professional experience as a licensed master HVAC mechanic, Jay Reed founded Jay's A/C and Heating LLC in 2008 to bring honest, reliable, father-and-son mechanical craftsmanship to Charles County.

Jay built his reputation on the principles of mechanical excellence: getting onto roofs, into crawl spaces, and beside condenser units to diagnose the true root cause of an HVAC malfunction before recommending a replacement.

Under his leadership, Jay's A/C and Heating LLC continues to operate as an authentic family business located on Logging Lane in Indian Head, Maryland. Whether you need an emergency repair during a heatwave or a straightforward assessment for a new heat pump, Jay and his team bring decades of hands-on technical skill to every service call.
